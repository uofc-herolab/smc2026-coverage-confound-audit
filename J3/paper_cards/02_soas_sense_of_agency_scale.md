# Paper Card — tapal2017_soas

## Full citation (APA)
- Tapal, A., Oren, E., Dar, R., & Eitam, B. (2017). The Sense of Agency Scale: A Measure of Consciously Perceived Control over One's Mind, Body, and the Immediate Environment. *Frontiers in Psychology, 8*, 1552.

## Link / DOI
- https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01552

## Problem (1–3 sentences)
- J3 needs a psychometrically validated instrument for *subjective sense of agency* to validate ALI as an objective proxy.
- Agency includes both feeling in control and feeling externally controlled, which may shift differently under autonomy.

## Approach (what they did)
- Developed and validated the Sense of Agency Scale (SoAS).
- Identified a two-factor structure separating positive agency (SoPA) and negative agency (SoNA).

## Setup / Participants / Data
- Psychometric validation cohorts (factor analysis + reliability + test-retest; see paper for sample details).

## Measures (what they measured)
- 11 items, 1–7 Likert (“strongly disagree” → “strongly agree”).
- Two subscales:
  - SoPA: positive agency
  - SoNA: negative agency / helplessness / “remote controlled robot” feeling

## Key results (include numbers)
- Reliability (reported):
  - SoPA: McDonald’s ω = 0.80; test-retest r = 0.78
  - SoNA: McDonald’s ω = 0.75; test-retest r = 0.74
- SoPA and SoNA are distinct (moderate negative correlation reported around r ≈ −0.38).

## Scoring methodology (J3-relevant)
- Score SoPA and SoNA separately (mean or sum per subscale).
- Do not collapse into a single overall agency score.

## Limitations / failure modes
- Trait-like framing; use per-block administration in J3 to capture condition differences.
- Requires clear participant instructions to anchor ratings to the interaction context.

## What we reuse (for J3)
- Primary subjective agency instrument for H1 (ALI ↔ SoAS).
- Use SoPA/SoNA to detect performance–agency decoupling.

## What we must NOT repeat (avoid redundancy vs C3/C1/C2)
- Don’t treat agency as one scalar number.
- Don’t ignore performance confounds; analyze SoAS with success/time covariates.
